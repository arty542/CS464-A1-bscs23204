### Mechanics, dynamics, aesthetics, and Bartle types

The dynamics below describe likely player behaviour caused by each rule.

| # | Mechanic | Dynamic | Aesthetic | Bartle type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Machines use ingredients and make one product at a time, with extra products queued in slots. [Production rules](https://ingame.help.supercellsupport.com/hay-day/en/articles/product-making-2.html) | Players fill queues before a break and return to collect the goods. | **Submission:** production fits into a regular routine. | **Achiever:** making goods helps finish orders. |
| M2 | Truck orders exchange set amounts of goods for coins and XP. Players can discard orders or ask for help. [Order rules](https://ingame.help.supercellsupport.com/hay-day/en/articles/truck-orders-2.html) | Players compare rewards, choose affordable orders, and ask for missing goods. | **Challenge:** choosing orders tests resource planning. | **Achiever:** orders grow the farm's coins and level. |
| M3 | Boat orders have crates to fill before the boat leaves, with extra rewards for a full shipment. [Boat rules](https://ingame.help.supercellsupport.com/hay-day/en/articles/boat-orders-3.html) | Players prepare goods early and ask others to help fill crates before time runs out. | **Challenge:** finishing the shipment creates time pressure. | **Achiever:** completing a boat gives a clear reward goal. |
| M4 | Different lures attract different fish; new fish and weight records earn Fishing Book rewards. [Fishing rules](https://ingame.help.supercellsupport.com/hay-day/en/articles/fishing-book.html) | Players try different lures and fishing spots to fill their book. | **Discovery:** each new catch adds something to find. | **Explorer:** experimenting reveals where and how to catch fish. |
| M5 | Expanding farm land uses set amounts of land deeds, mallets, and marker stakes. [Expansion rules](https://ingame.help.supercellsupport.com/hay-day/en/articles/land-expansion.html) | Players save the needed supplies and choose which plot to unlock next. | **Challenge:** balancing supplies makes expansion a longer goal. | **Achiever:** each unlocked plot shows progress. |
| M6 | Pets such as cats, dogs, and horses eat food; using the whistle after feeding collects XP and can give supplies. [Pet rules](https://ingame.help.supercellsupport.com/hay-day/en/articles/farm-animals.html) | Players keep food ready and return to feed and wake their pets. | **Fantasy:** caring for pets adds to the feeling of owning a farm. | **Achiever:** pet care also earns XP and useful items. |
| M7 | Neighborhood event tasks award points toward shared rewards, with a personal contribution target. | Neighbors choose tasks they can finish and work toward the group goal. | **Fellowship:** progress depends on working together. | **Socializer:** the shared goal encourages group participation. |

**Aesthetic profile:** Submission comes from the production routine (M1). Challenge comes from orders, boat deadlines, and expansion supplies (M2, M3, M5). Fellowship comes from helping with orders and working on neighborhood goals (M2, M3, M7).

**Player types**

- **Primary: Achiever - acting on the world.** Players make goods, finish orders, collect supplies, and expand their farm (M1-M3, M5, M6).
- **Secondary: Socializer - interacting with players.** Order help and shared neighborhood goals give farmers reasons to work together (M2, M3, M7).

## Level blockouts

These five levels are inspired by the floating buildings and bubbles in the movie *Bubble*. They share a prefab kit in `Assets/Prefabs/`.

Blue capsule = start. Blue bubbles = jump points. Green beacon = goal. Green floor marks = breadcrumbs.

Open `Level01` to `Level05` in `Assets/Scenes/` to view them in Unity. These are static blockouts with no movement scripts.

Walk on the building roofs and use bubbles to cross gaps. The short route has a sideways-tilted roof and walls to move around. The longer route goes around the outer buildings. Both reach the same goal.
